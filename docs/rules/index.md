## The core roll

*TBD.* What do you pick up, what do you add, and what counts as success?

Worth pinning down early, because everything downstream inherits it:

- **Dice.** One die, a pool, a fixed spread? Does the shape of the curve match
  the tone you want — swingy or grindy?
- **Target numbers.** Fixed difficulty ladder, opposed rolls, or both?
- **Degrees of outcome.** Pass/fail only, or margins that produce partial
  successes and complications?
- **Player-facing or GM-facing.** Does the referee ever roll?
